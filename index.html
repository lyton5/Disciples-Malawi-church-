About Disciples Malawi

Disciples Malawi is a Christian ministry serving people and communities in Malawi. Our desire is to encourage people through the Word of God, worship, fellowship and Christian service.

Our Beginning

Disciples Malawi started in Malawi at Chitipa Boma, near Nachiwe Primary School, through a group of people who came together with a desire to serve God and reach others.

Our Ministry

We seek to share Christian teaching, encourage believers, build fellowship and serve our communities through the ministry entrusted to us.

Worship & Fellowship

We believe that fellowship and worship help believers grow together in faith, love and service.

Growing Together

Everyone is welcome to learn, worship, fellowship and grow together as part of the Christian community.

Our Purpose

To encourage people to know God, grow in faith, live according to Christian teaching and serve others with love.
